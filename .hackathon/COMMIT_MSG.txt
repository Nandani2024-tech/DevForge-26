feat(R7,R9): apply run-time name evidence to plain-text documents

Nursing notes, prescriptions and discharge texts were cleaned by the
rule-based detectors only, so a name written between a time stamp and
a clinical clause survived even when the same name was harvested from
the run's structured files. The Pass 1 name evidence now also runs on
plain-text documents, inside the same R8 protect mask the rule-based
detectors use, so doses, wards, doctors and clinical terms stay intact.

Changes:
- pipeline: plain-text documents take the run's name evidence as a
  required input and apply it after the rule-based cleaner
- channels: evidence redaction skips every R8 protected span; single
  words only in documents that belong to a patient, full-name phrases
  elsewhere
- names: protect mask exported; the health-worker abbreviation stays
  protected except right after a person label or before a surname
- tests: nursing-note, protected-span, patient-gate, all-caps label and
  whole-pipeline text scaling cases with invented names

  medical kept 4854/4855, F0.5 0.96, 0 wrong merges

  0.32/2.71/33.58 s, near linear)
